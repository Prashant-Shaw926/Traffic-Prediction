3. Simulation-engine workflow
run_simulation(timestamp) calls, in order:
- build_simulation_input
- discover_relationships on that same window
- calculate_traffic_influence on that same window and those relationships
- load_spatial_artifacts once if the existing cache is empty
- predict_request once per supported junction

4. Spatial GRU service reused
backend.services.spatial_prediction_service.predict_request
Model identifier returned by the service: Spatial_GRU
Features: 53
Lookback: 168
historical_simulation: true
The engine does not retrain the model and does not call the baseline GRU.

5. Separate outputs
Predicted traffic is the Spatial GRU predicted_vehicles value.
Historical traffic is the raw observation at T when that hour exists.
Relationship strength remains the Stage 2 signed and absolute correlation.
Influence index remains the Stage 3 formula output.
These fields are not substituted for each other.

7. Timestamp validation and leakage checks
Malformed timestamps and 2017-07-01 10:00 raise the existing Stage 1 SimulationInputError before inference.
Every relationship window ends at the requested timestamp.
Influence uses that same timestamp.
Stage 1 observations used for relationships and influence are at or before T.
Vehicles(T) is reported as historical traffic only. It is not an engine-built model input.
Repeated 2017-06-15 10:00 runs match exactly for relationships and influence, and within 1e-6 for predictions.
SHA-256 of the spatial model, both spatial scalers, and feature_config.json was unchanged after the tests.

11. Known limitations
- There is no Flask route and no React view for this engine.
- The influence index is not a vehicle forecast and does not prove physical causation.
- Lag 168 remains a candidate, but the 168-hour window still contains no aligned pairs for that lag.
- predict_request is called once per junction because that is the existing single-junction interface.

12. Frozen pipeline
Model weights, scalers, feature configuration, preprocessing, the spatial prediction service, /api/predict, and the React frontend were not changed. No model was retrained.
