Stage 3 formula-based dynamic traffic influence
Generated from the Stage 3, Stage 2, and Stage 1 test run.
The influence score is a mathematical index. It is not a predicted vehicle count and it is not evidence that one junction physically causes traffic at another.

3. Formula and variable definitions
I_j(T) = sum over sources i of [W_ij(T) * V_i(T - k_ij)]

I_j(T) is the incoming influence index for target junction j at simulation timestamp T.
k_ij is the lag selected by Stage 2 for that ordered pair.
V_i(T - k_ij) is the source junction's observed vehicle count at that exact hour.
The sum uses only relationships whose exact source hour exists in the Stage 1 window.

4. Weight definition
W_ij(T) is the Stage 2 absolute_correlation. The signed correlation is copied onto each contribution as signed_correlation and is not used as the weight. Stage 2's selection rule was not changed.

7. Missing-observation handling
If T - lag_hours is not an exact observation for the source junction, that contribution is omitted and recorded with reason "missing exact source observation". It is not interpolated, replaced by a nearby row, or entered as zero. An isolated fixture confirmed a present contribution of 5.0 stayed 5.0 when a second source hour was missing. Both real timestamps above had every required source hour, so excluded_contributions was empty.

8. Leakage prevention checks
Every included source timestamp equals T - lag_hours and is at or before T.
Source traffic matches the raw historical row at that exact timestamp and junction.
Relationship discovery still uses only the Stage 1 window ending at T.
Target traffic at T is not used as a substitute for the lagged source observation.
Repeating 2017-06-15 10:00 returns an identical result.
2017-07-01 10:00 and a malformed timestamp still raise the Stage 1 SimulationInputError.

11. Limitations
- The index is not a vehicle forecast and does not prove physical causation.
- Both tested windows selected only positive correlations, so the stored weight equals the signed correlation numerically. A negative signed correlation would still be preserved, and the weight would be its absolute value.
- A relationship whose exact lagged hour is outside the Stage 1 window, including an unused lag of 168, cannot contribute.
- No strength threshold is applied. Every selected Stage 2 relationship with an exact source observation is included.

12. Frozen pipeline
