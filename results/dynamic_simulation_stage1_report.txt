Simulation input definition
- SimulationRequest: one hour-aligned timestamp.
- SimulationWindow: closed interval [T - 167 hours, T] and one JunctionTrafficState per supported junction.
- JunctionTrafficState: junction id plus chronological TrafficObservation(timestamp, vehicles) copied from data/raw/traffic.csv.
- SimulationData: request, window, and the SimulationConfig used to build them.
- SimulationConfig defaults: historical_window_hours=168, supported_junctions=(1, 2, 3, 4), supported_lags=(1, 2, 3, 24, 168), minimum_observations=24, dataset_end=2017-06-30 23:00:00.
- RelationshipSettings.minimum_paired_observations=24 is stored only. Stage 1 does not compute pairs or weights.

Historical window behavior
- The window ends at the requested timestamp T and starts at T - (historical_window_hours - 1) hours.
- Each supported junction must have a real row at T and at least minimum_observations real rows inside the window.
- Missing hours stay missing. regularize_hourly() is not called.

Validation rules
- Malformed timestamp: "Invalid datetime."
- Non-hourly timestamp: "Datetime must be aligned to a full hour."
- After dataset_end or after the latest raw timestamp: "The historical dataset ends at 2017-06-30 23:00 and does not contain sufficient data for this timestamp."
- Missing endpoint or too few real rows: "Insufficient historical data for the requested simulation timestamp."

Leakage prevention
- Rows with DateTime > T are removed before the window is sliced.
- The builder asserts that the cutoff and the sliced window contain no future timestamps.
- Vehicle counts are floats from the raw CSV after dropping unparseable rows and duplicate (Junction, DateTime) keys. No values are interpolated.
